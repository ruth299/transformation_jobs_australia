# Transformation Jobs Australia

A curated dashboard for Transformation, Strategy, Delivery, Architecture and AI jobs across Australia.

## Features

- Australia-wide job search
  - Melbourne
  - Sydney
  - Brisbane
  - Perth
  - Canberra
  - Adelaide

- Transformation-focused roles
  - Business Analysis
  - Project Management
  - Program Management
  - Architecture
  - Strategy
  - AI & Digital Transformation
  - ERP & Enterprise Systems

- Sector filtering
  - Government
  - Higher Education
  - Healthcare
  - Banking & Finance
  - Utilities & Energy
  - Technology

- Live market statistics
  - Total jobs
  - New jobs today
  - Average salary
  - Top source
  - Last refreshed

- Sector breakdown chart

- Dark / Light mode

## About

Transformation Jobs Australia is a curated job board focused on transformation, project and program delivery, business analysis, architecture, strategy and AI opportunities across Australia.

The site brings together opportunities from across the market to help transformation professionals discover relevant roles more efficiently.

Curated by Ruth Sequeira | Transformation & Program Delivery Professional
